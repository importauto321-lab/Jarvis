# Gemini
Wired in: yes, iPad page (version 52). Not yet tried with the real service.

## Does well
- Live Google search research for current information such as rates, news and prices.
- Watching and summarizing YouTube videos.
- Reading long documents, PDFs and whole books.
- Reading long email threads.
- Understanding pictures and screenshots.
- Reading receipts and invoices and pulling the data out of them.
- Listening to audio and transcribing it.

## Rules
- Send these to Gemini whenever the user asks, and tell the user it was sent.
- Free plan. Google may use what is sent on the free plan, so keep sensitive documents out.
- Files up to about 14 MB.
